# MMG: request approval workflow (approver guide)

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: approvers (MMG) · Source file: Request Approval Workflow MMG.pptx · Ingested: 2026-09-12

How an approver logs in and responds to pending approval requests on app.procol.in.

## Login

1. Log in through app.procol.in.
2. Enter your registered email ID or phone number.
3. Enter the OTP received on your email ID or phone number.
4. You are logged in.

## Find pending approvals

5. Open the Approval tab from the left-hand sidebar.
6. The "Pending on Me" tab lists all approvals currently pending with you.

## Respond to one or many approvals

7. Select multiple approvals at once and click "Add Response".
8. Approve or reject the request as required and provide "Approval Remarks" before submitting.

## Review event details before approving

9. To review the event before approving, click the "Event Title" to open the event details.
10. Scroll down to review the details and compare all vendor quotations before approving the request.
11. Once everything is in order, provide "Remarks" on the right-hand side and click "Approve".
