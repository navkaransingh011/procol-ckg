# Counter offers: single and bulk, buyer and supplier sides

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: buyers and suppliers · Source file: Counter Offer Module.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

Learn how to navigate the Procol platform to submit, modify and accept counter offers during an RFQ. This guide covers the steps for adjusting bid details and managing bulk communications in procurement negotiations.

## Counter offer during RFQ submission time

1. During the RFQ submission time the buyer can send a counter offer to a vendor after the vendor has submitted its technical and RFQ quotation.
2. Open the vendor's quote and click "Counter Offer".
3. A counter offer can be sent on Quantity Offered, Rate, Delivery Time, Incoterms and Additional Charges.
4. Edit the fields you want to counter (for example Quantity Offered, Rate, Incoterms such as ASW - At Supplier's Works).
5. Click "Send Counter Offer". The quote now shows "Counter offer requested".

## Supplier response during submission time

6. The supplier receives a notification.
7. While the RFQ is still in submission time the supplier can only Accept or Decline the counter offer; modifying is not available.
8. Once the supplier clicks Accept, the buyer sees that the counter offer is accepted.

## Counter offer during evaluation time

9. When the event is in evaluation time and the buyer sends a counter offer, the supplier can Accept, Modify or Decline it.
10. The buyer can use "Bulk Update" on the bid sheet to set the counter values for a line item (the sheet shows the L1 bid, quantity requested and offered, last PO price and rate).
11. Click "Send Counter Offer". The vendor receives a notification such as "AMNS sent you a counter offer in RFX-21105 | RFQ | 10079-HAND TOOLS. Review and respond at the earliest."
12. To modify, the supplier clicks Modify, edits the fields, enters a Reason in the Remarks field and clicks "Place Modified Bid".
13. The buyer can see that the supplier has modified the counter offer.

## Bulk counter offer

14. From the RFQ stage the buyer can also "Send Bulk Counter Offer" to multiple suppliers at once.
15. Set the counter values and click "Send Counter Offer".
16. Beneath each supplier's name the buyer can see whether the counter offer was accepted, rejected or modified.
