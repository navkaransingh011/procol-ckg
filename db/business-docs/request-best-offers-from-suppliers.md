# Requesting best offers from suppliers

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: buyers (and the vendor response side) · Source file: HowtoRequestBestOffersfromSuppliers.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

This guide walks you through the process of requesting and revising competitive bids from your suppliers within the Procol platform. Learn how to manage counter offers and update your bid sheets efficiently to secure the best pricing for your projects.

## Open the RFQ stage settings

1. Navigate to your portal and go to the event and click on RFQ stage.
2. Click "Settings"

## Request Best Offers

3. Click "Request Best Offers"
4. Select duration of the best offer time during which the vendors can provide their best prices.
5. Select the required participating vendors using the header checkbox to whom you wish request best offer.
6. Click the "Enter Remarks" field.
7. Click "Send Request"

## Vendor Response

8. Vendor can see against the event that there is "B.O. Requested"
9. Open the requested best-offer event and scroll the product sheet towards the right.
10. Enter the revised rate under the "Rate" column. After editing vendor needs to click on "Revise Quote"

## Review Supplier Offers

11. After the best offer time ends the buyer can see the response of the best offer , beneath the name of the vendor .
