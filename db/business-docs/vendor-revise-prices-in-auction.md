# Vendors: revising prices in an auction and messaging the buyer

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: vendors / suppliers on trade.procol.in · Source file: How Vendors Can Revise Prices in an Auction.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

Step-by-Step Guide Learn how vendors can revise their prices during an auction, ensuring they remain competitive throughout the process. It also covers how vendors can raise queries or seek clarifications using the message section, enabling smooth communication and participation in the event.

## Find the event

1. Navigate to https://trade.procol.in
2. Event will be visible to you under "Active" tab, click here to view the same.
3. Click "Details" to view published stages, contact info of the buyer, line item details.

## RFQ stage: the rates you already submitted

4. Click here to view the "RFQ" stage.
5. Click "Proceed"
6. In this stage, your rates will be visible which you have shared with the buyer.

## Auction stage: revise your bid

7. Click here to view the "Auction" stage where you can revise your prices.
8. In this stage, you will be able to view the order submission time. Please make sure to quote during the submission time to avoid any delays.
9. Click "Rate" to enter your revised quote.
10. You may enter other details as per your requirement.
11. Enter "Remarks" if any.
12. Click "Revise Bid"

## Messages: raise a query or clarification with the buyer

13. Your bid will be successfully revised. If you wish to raise queries or seek clarifications, you may click "Messages" to direct communicate with the buyer.
14. Click "Message"
15. Click here to select the message query as required.
16. Click the line item for which you wish to raise the query or seek clarification.
17. Click the "Describe your query here" field, and add the details.
18. Click "Send Message"
19. You message will be sent to the buyer and you may view the same here.

## Rank visibility

20. Also, when you input your rates, your "Rank" will be visible to you and you may revise your prices accordingly.
