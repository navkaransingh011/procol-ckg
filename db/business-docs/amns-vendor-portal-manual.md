# AMNS vendor portal user manual (trade.procol.in)

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: vendors / suppliers of AM/NS India · Source file: AMNS-Vendor.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to navigate and use the vendor platform for AMNS events: logging in, submitting the intent to participate, filling the technical and RFQ stages, messaging the buyer and bidding in the auction stage.

## Logging in

1. Navigate to https://trade.procol.in
2. To sign in, enter your registered phone number and click "Send OTP".
3. Enter the OTP received on the registered contact number and log in to the platform.

## Event page overview

4. Suppliers can check the status of events in the three tabs: Live, History and All events.

## Intent for participation

5. Details of the event are visible to the supplier, who then selects either to participate or to decline.

## Submission for the technical stage

6. In the Technical stage tab, if the technical specifications are acceptable (or to regret), the vendor can select the quick-fill option from the dropdown; the values remain editable.
7. The vendor can submit remarks related to the event and click to submit the proposal.

## Submission of the RFQ stage

8. Select the RFQ tab to participate and choose the currency to proceed.
9. Submit the price offer and the other parameters, then submit the quote.

## Messaging tool

10. For concerns or clarifications the vendor can open the Messages section.
11. Select the options in the dropdowns and click "Send message".
12. The buyer's reply appears in the same thread.

## Auction stage

13. To participate in the bidding, select the Reverse (auction) option.
14. Vendors can revise their prices and submit bids according to their displayed rank.
15. The vendor's current position (rank) is shown on the right side of the screen.
