# Standalone auction: surrogate bids and converting an RFQ to an auction

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: buyers · Source file: Complete Guide to Creating a Standalone.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

Auction Learn a step-by-step walkthrough on creating a standalone auction, including how to add surrogate bidders and convert an RFQ into an auction. It covers the complete process to help users seamlessly set up and manage auctions on the platform.

## Create a single-stage RFQ event

1. Navigate to https://app.procol.in
2. Click "Events"
3. Click "Single Stage"
4. Click here to select the "Event Type"
5. Click "RFQ"
6. Click "Save"

## Fill the commercial template

7. Select this template "commercial for stand alone"
8. Select "Product"
9. Add "Delivery Location"
10. Add "Description" as per your requirement
11. Add "Quantity Requested"
12. Enter the "Event Title" as required

## Add vendors, schedule and publish

13. Click here to add vendors as per your requirement.
14. Click here to select the "Schedule"
15. You may click here to select the "Start time" or "Schedule For Later" as required.
16. Click "Save Schedule"
17. Click "Publish Event"
18. You may check or uncheck this as per your requirement.
19. Click "Publish"
20. Event will be published and you may view the "RFQ" details by clicking here.

## Add surrogate bids on behalf of vendors

21. To add "Surrogate Bids", Click here on "Participants"
22. Click this button to view the participants of this event.
23. Click "Add Bid"
24. Enter "Rate". Also, the GST fields have now been made non-mandatory. For cases where GST input credit is applicable, you may choose to exclude it and proceed with the total value without GST. For indirect purchases, GST can still be added as required.
25. You may add other details as required.
26. Click "Submit Quote"
27. Same way, add "Surrogate Bids" for other vendors too. To view their bids, Click "Responses"
28. Click this button to change the layout for better understanding.
29. All the bids added will be visible here.

## Convert the RFQ to an auction (sent to the Auction team)

30. Now click "Settings"
31. Click "Convert to Auction"
32. Click "Continue"
33. Enter the title of the project as required.
34. Click here to select the timings of the event.
35. Click "Save Schedule"
36. Click "Send to Auction team"
37. Click "Send to Auction Team"
38. Once the Auction team publishes the event from their end, you may click here to view the same.
39. Click this button to change the layout.
40. Bids added during the "RFQ" will be visible here.
