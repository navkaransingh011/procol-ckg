# Vendor awarding and PO creation

Status: current · Last reviewed: 2026-09 · Audience: buyers · Source file: Vendor Awarding & PO Creation Process.pdf · Ingested: 2026-09-12

A step-by-step guide covering the vendor awarding process, creating and submitting the PO, submitting proposals, and viewing the required details after awarding.

## Award vendors from the Master QCS

1. Navigate to your portal and open the event where you wish to proceed with awarding.
2. Click "Master QCS"
3. Click "Start Awarding". Also, please make sure that the latest commercial stage is in evaluation period, since it is required for further proceeding with the awarding process.
4. You may either scroll towards the right to view the vendors and select for awarding or you may change the layout of the page by clicking here.
5. Select the vendors for the particular line item for awarding.
6. Click "Award", same goes for the other line items too.
7. To view the consolidate summary of the awarded vendors, click here.
8. In this awarded vendor list, the details of the vendor, line items awarded along with the total proposal value would be visible for easy identification.

## Create the PO

9. Click "Create PO"
10. Click Quick Fill to select all the required details at once instead of selecting them individually for each line item.
11. Open the tax code field for all items.
12. Once both the details are selected via the dropdown, Click "Apply to All"
13. Now, in this PO form, please make sure all the required details are filled.
14. Once all the mandatory details are entered, click "Submit Proposal"

## Select the approval workflow and submit the proposal

15. Click "Select Approval Workflow"
16. The clauses available in the dropdown depend on the event creator’s team and the total proposal value. Select the required clause from the list.
17. Click "Next"
18. Review the details here.
19. Mention the Closing Remarks or note for approval.
20. Review the approval workflow approvers.
21. Click "Submit Proposal"

## NFA approval

22. Once submitted, the NFA will move in progress. To view the same, click here.
23. Open the approval tab, the approver details would be visible and they may enter the remarks and further approve the same.
24. The approver will also be having the visibility of this page, where they may view all the order related details and further take necessary actions.
25. Once the approver approves the NFA, it will reflect the same as below and if they wish to communicate any details to any user, they may click on Comments.
26. Tag any user here and enter the remarks as required.

## PO generation, failures and retry

27. Once the NFA is approver, the PO is either generated or failed, to view the same, click on the latest commercial stage and click here.
28. If the order is failed, click here to view the error description behind the failure.
29. After making the necessary updates according to the error description, you may click on save and retry below.
